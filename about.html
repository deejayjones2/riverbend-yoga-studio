About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Instructors

Our instructors welcome students of different backgrounds and experience levels. They focus on clear guidance, supportive modifications, and respectful instruction.

Accessibility and Accommodations

We encourage students to share mobility, sensory, or other access needs before attending. Ask about class modifications and available accommodations so we can help you plan your visit.

Studio Policies

Arrive a little early, silence your phone, respect others' space, and let your instructor know if you need to take a break. Wear comfortable clothing and bring water.
